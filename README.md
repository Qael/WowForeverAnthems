# WoW Forever Anthems

These are free songs for the WoW Forever community. I made them for the PvP videos, battlegrounds and late-night world PvP that keep this game alive, and I hope you enjoy them.

Use them however you like: PvP videos, streams, montages, guild events, anything.

If you do use one, a shout-out to WoW Forever Anthems on YouTube and a link back to this repo would mean a lot. It's never required, though.

New songs are added here as they premiere on the WoW Forever Anthems YouTube channel.

## Submit your clips

Made a PvP video, montage or stream highlight with one of these songs? We'd love to see it. Share it in the **Submit your clips** category of [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Qael/WowForeverAnthems/discussions). Include a link to your clip, the song you used and the name you'd like credited. Favorite clips may be featured on the WoW Forever Anthems YouTube channel, always with credit to you.
